Installation Walkthrough

Install In Chrome or Edge in 60 Seconds

Follow these five simple steps to load the unpacked extension in developer mode.

1. Download & Authenticate

Click “Download .zip package” and sign in with Google to register your developer account in Neon DB and download the Snipy extension archive (snipy-extension.zip).

2. Extract / Unzip Archive

Go to your Downloads folder, right-click the downloaded snipy-extension.zip file, and extract or unzip it into a folder on your computer.

3. Open Extensions Management Page

In Google Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions/, or in Microsoft Edge, navigate to edge://extensions/ in your address bar and press Enter.

4. Toggle Developer Mode

Turn on the Developer mode toggle switch in the top-right corner of the browser extensions manager page.

5. Load Unpacked Folder

Click the “Load unpacked” button in the top-left area, select the folder where you extracted the Snipy extension files, and click Select Folder. You are ready to optimize!
